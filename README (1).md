# MASTA UniversityOS

### The digital operating and intelligence layer for modern universities

MASTA UniversityOS connects students, lecturers, registry, finance, and university management around one shared academic system. It is designed to support the full student journey—from admission and registration to results, transcripts, graduation, and alumni—while giving staff and leadership tools to coordinate operations and make better decisions.

**[Explore the live demo →](https://masta-university-os.vercel.app/dashboard)**

> **One platform · One student record · One institutional view**

## Overview

University operations often depend on disconnected systems, spreadsheets, and manual approvals. MASTA UniversityOS brings academic, financial, and administrative workflows into one shared digital environment.

The platform's long-term goal is to help universities operate more efficiently, improve the student experience, and turn institutional data into useful intelligence.

## Who Uses MASTA?

| User | Main experience |
| --- | --- |
| **Students** | Timetables, course registration, results, transcripts, documents, lesson materials, fees, and notifications. |
| **Lecturers** | Teaching timetables, attendance, assignments, course materials, class records, and grade submission. |
| **Registry / Admin** | Student records, academic scheduling, programme structure, timetable publishing, and academic workflows. |
| **Finance** | Fees, payments, balances, receipts, and collection monitoring. |
| **University Management** | Institutional dashboards, performance indicators, student trends, and decision intelligence. |

## Platform Scope

The following describes the platform's intended scope. Availability and functionality may vary in the current demo.

### Academic Structure

Manage sessions, semesters, faculties, departments, programmes, levels, and courses within a coordinated academic framework.

### Timetables

Registry creates, edits, and publishes official schedules. Students and lecturers automatically see timetables relevant to their academic records or teaching assignments.

### Student Records

Maintain one continuous digital student record containing academic, financial, and administrative information throughout the student journey.

### Teaching and Assessment

Support attendance, assignments, lesson materials, grades, approvals, and results within connected teaching workflows.

### Documents and Transcripts

Give students access to official documents and pathways to generate or request academic transcripts.

### Finance

Bring student charges, payments, balances, and receipts into the shared university environment.

### Institutional Intelligence

Provide dashboards and analytics to help university leaders understand performance, identify emerging issues, and make informed decisions.

## How Timetables Work

### For Students

Students do not manually choose a level timetable. The platform uses each student's:

- Programme
- Level
- Academic session
- Semester
- Registered courses

These details determine the correct schedule to display.

**Example:** Amina Bello, a 300-level B.Sc. Computer Science student in the first semester of the 2026/2027 session, sees the timetable relevant to her academic profile and registered courses.

### For Lecturers

Lecturer timetables are generated from teaching assignments, including courses across different levels or departments.

**Example: Dr. Ibrahim's teaching schedule**

| Day | Course | Level |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Monday | CSC301 | 300 |
| Tuesday | SEN205 | 200 |
| Thursday | CSC401 | 400 |

## The Student Journey

MASTA's intended student lifecycle covers:

**Applicant → Admission → Student → Courses → Results → Graduation → Alumni**

A connected record supports continuity between stages and gives the institution a clearer view of each student's journey.
